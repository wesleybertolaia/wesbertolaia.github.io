Design & evidence

Opportunity
Patients need to know who reviews their request and what submitting it commits them to.
What shipped
Two live entry paths share the same intake: a one-question guide and a direct start. Both end in a trackable request.
What I verified
Browser checks covered submitting, reopening and withdrawing a sample request.
How I’d test it
Compare sample submissions per landing visit. Ask users what happens next to check whether the guide improves understanding.

Opportunity
A patient can mistake a requested time for a confirmed appointment and make plans too early.
What shipped
Two headlines lead to the same booking flow. Patients choose a time before entering sample details and can track the clinic’s decision.
What I verified
I tested a clinic decline, a patient retry and the updated status in the patient view.
How I’d test it
Compare requests per landing visit across headlines. After submission, ask users whether their appointment is confirmed.

Opportunity
When a shift comes back with an unclear rejection, employees have to guess what to fix.
What shipped
Managers return a shift with a specific reason. Employees correct and resubmit it. Approved entries can be exported.
What I verified
I tested returning a shift for a break correction, approving the resubmission and checking the exported minutes.
How I’d test it
Give users a returned shift with an incorrect break. Measure correct resubmissions and time to fix the entry.
Opportunity
Pet owners comparing overnight stays need to know whether the service suits their animal and what the full stay costs.
What shipped
Dog and cat owners see different care options and prices. The booking shows the total for the selected nights before they confirm.
What I verified
I checked the price for a two-night cat stay and confirmed the booking could be reopened and cancelled after reload.
How I’d test it
Compare an upfront nightly rate with pricing shown at review. Track completed bookings per service view and whether owners can correctly predict the total.
Opportunity
A neighbourhood has a fixed budget, but the options that save the most money aren’t always the ones that cut peak demand.
What shipped
Visitors build a plan, pin it and see how a different allocation changes three outcomes. The assumptions are visible.
What I verified
The simulator enforces the budget and uses illustrative values for a fictional neighbourhood.
How I’d test it
Ask users to pick a plan for a peak-demand goal, then explain what they gave up to reach it.
