Healthcare acquisition

CarePath

Problem

Who reviews my request, and what happens after I start? Those questions can stop someone before they open the form.

Design decision

The guided route answers one question the visitor chooses. The direct route opens the same form preview immediately.

How I’d test it

Compare how many visitors reach the form preview and finish the demo in each route. Use drop-off at the question step to see whether guidance earns the extra click.

Patient booking

DentalDesk

Problem

A patient needs to know if a time fits their schedule and whether it’s confirmed before they can plan around it.

Design decision

The booking flow shows sample slots before asking for contact details. The review step explains that a request still needs clinic confirmation.

How I’d test it

Test “See dental visit times” against “Request a dental visit” with the same booking flow. Compare requests per landing session. In a real clinic, check confirmed visits too.

Employee onboarding

ShiftLedger

Problem

An 8-hour shift with a 30-minute unpaid break should count as 7h30. A wrong entry also changes the manager’s total.

Design decision

New users can load an editable example. The form checks the time range and break before adding net hours to the manager view.

How I’d test it

Compare the share of new users who save a correct first shift with an example versus a blank form. In a task using different hours, check how often people save the sample unchanged.
