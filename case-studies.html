Healthcare acquisition

CarePath

Friction

Visitors may want to know who reviews their request or what happens before taking the first step.

What I built

One variant starts at intake. The other lets visitors pick a question first. Both reach the same intake preview and log the same start event.

What I’d measure

Intake starts per eligible landing session. Track guided-step drop-off and completed intakes to see whether the guidance helps.

Patient booking · Angular

DentalDesk

Friction

Patients may hesitate to share details before seeing available times and knowing when the clinic will confirm.

What I built

A guided request that shows sample times before review. I tested that the visit type and time are kept when a visitor goes back.

What I’d measure

Submitted requests per eligible session for a request-first offer versus a time-first offer. Check confirmed visits as a follow-up.

Employee onboarding · React

ShiftLedger

Friction

New employees may be unsure how to log breaks. Managers need a clear view of recorded time.

What I built

A first-shift form with editable examples and validation. In a browser test, saving the example moved the manager total from 24h 30m to 26h.

What I’d measure

Valid first saves per new employee session. Check time to save, errors, and accidental acceptance of the example.
